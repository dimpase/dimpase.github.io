NeoMutt Gmail

This is Dima Pasechnik's personal setup for using the NeoMutt email client with his own Gmail account. The OAuth application is maintained by Dima Pasechnik and is intended only for his personal use.

What it does

NeoMutt runs on the owner's computer and connects directly to Gmail to read, compose, send, organize, and delete email. It uses Google's OAuth sign-in and encrypted connections for IMAP and SMTP.

The requested https://mail.google.com/ permission allows access to email, including sending and permanently deleting messages. Mailbox operations are performed through the local email client.

Where the data goes

Email is exchanged directly between the owner's computer and Google's mail servers. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Local message copies, drafts, attachments, and caches may be retained according to the email client's settings and the owner's actions.

This website provides information about the setup. It has no sign-in form and does not receive email, Google passwords, or OAuth tokens.

See the privacy policy for data handling and revocation details, and the terms of use for the scope of this personal setup.
