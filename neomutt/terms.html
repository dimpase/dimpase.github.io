Terms of use

Personal use

NeoMutt Gmail is an OAuth configuration maintained by Dima Pasechnik solely for accessing his own Gmail account from his local NeoMutt installation. It is not offered as a public email service or an application for other users to connect their accounts.

Account access

Access requires the account owner's authorization through Google. The owner controls the client's mailbox actions, including sending, moving, and deleting messages, and is responsible for protecting the computer, local files, and authorization credentials.

Software and services

NeoMutt and the OAuth helper remain subject to their respective software licenses. Gmail and Google authorization remain subject to Google's applicable terms. This personal configuration is maintained independently of Google and the NeoMutt project.

Stopping use

The owner can stop using the setup at any time, revoke access through Google Account connections, and remove local credentials and stored email as desired. Details are provided in the privacy policy.
