Privacy policy

This policy describes the NeoMutt Gmail OAuth application maintained by Dima Pasechnik for access to his own Gmail account.

Data accessed and its purpose

The local email client accesses the account's email address, messages, message headers, sender and recipient addresses, attachments, folders, labels, and message status as needed to read, compose, send, organize, and delete email. OAuth access and refresh tokens authorize these connections without giving the client the Google account password.

The full mail permission permits reading, sending, changing, and permanently deleting messages through Gmail's IMAP and SMTP interfaces. Data is used only to provide these personal email functions.

Storage and protection

The client connects directly to Google over encrypted connections. OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's computer in a file encrypted with GPG. Email, attachments, drafts, and caches may also be stored locally according to client settings and the owner's actions; these local files are not necessarily encrypted by the email client.

There is no separate application server that collects or stores mailbox data. This website and its public source repository contain no mailbox data or OAuth credentials.

Sharing and other uses

The setup exchanges data with Google to provide email functionality. Messages and attachments are delivered to recipients when the owner sends or forwards them. This setup does not otherwise sell or share Google user data, use it for advertising or analytics, or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Local tokens are retained until removed or replaced. The owner can revoke this application's access through Google Account connections and delete the local token file. Revoking access stops further authorized access but does not delete messages already stored in Gmail or downloaded to the computer.

The owner controls retention and deletion of local messages, drafts, attachments, caches, and backups. Gmail retains server-side email according to the account's settings and Google's policies.

This informational website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor connection data, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting these pages does not authorize access to a Google account.
